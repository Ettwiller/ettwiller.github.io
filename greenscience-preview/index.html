Propose research that makes NEB more sustainable

The Green Science Program funds one employee idea each cycle that makes NEB's processes, research or operations, and beyond, more sustainable. The winning project gets a summer student who works on it alongside Dan and Levi in the Sustainability department.

Accepting proposals Deadline 15 Feb 2027 (83 days left)

How it works

  1. Submit a proposal Describe your proposed research, the environmental benefit you expect (1–2 pages) and what the student would do.
  2. Committee review Every proposal is scored against the criteria on this page.
  3. Award The winning project gets a summer student, working with you and with Dan and Levi in Sustainability.
  4. Report Share a short report comparing the new process with the current one.

Review criteria

  • Scientific feasibility Is the approach sound and doable with the resources requested?
  • Environmental benefit How large and how measurable is the expected gain?
  • Alignment with NEB strategy Does it fit NEB's operations, products and sustainability goals?
  • Cross-department collaboration Does it bring people from different departments together?
  • Fit for a summer student Is there a clear, well-mentored project for about 10-12 weeks?
