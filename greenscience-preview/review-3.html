Reusing insulated shipping boxes and recovering dry ice

Chris Alvarez · chris.alvarez@neb.com · Distribution · Logistics Coordinator

Submitted 24 Nov 2026 · last edited 24 Nov 2026

Submitted

Proposal

Every cold-chain shipment leaves in an insulated box with dry ice. Many local customers would return the boxes, and returned boxes often still hold usable dry ice. We propose a return-and-reuse pilot with ten high-volume customers in New England: prepaid return labels, inspection criteria for reuse, and a log of how many boxes come back in reusable condition. We would also measure how much dry ice can be recovered from returns.

What the student would do

Coordinate the pilot with customers, inspect and log returned boxes, measure recovered dry ice, and calculate the packaging and CO2 savings.
